# 🥗 Healthcare Diet & Calories Intake Dashboard

An interactive Power BI data visualization project designed to analyze health metrics, dietary types, calorie consumption, water intake, and physical activity levels across diverse demographics.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Metrics

* **Demographic Overview:** Tracks total individuals analyzed, average BMI ($23.63$), average water intake ($2.85\text{L}$), and average weight ($62.96\text{ kg}$)[cite: 2].
* **Dietary Analysis:** Breakdown of population distribution across multiple diet types (Balanced, Mediterranean, Vegetarian, High Protein, Vegan, and Keto)[cite: 2].
* **Nutrient & Caloric Intake:** Evaluates average protein, carbohydrate, and fat intake grouped by specific dietary preferences alongside average daily calorie requirements vs. actual consumption[cite: 2].
* **Health Status & Activity Level:** Cross-references health conditions (Healthy, Obese, Overweight, Underweight) with user activity levels (Moderately Active, Lightly Active, Sedentary, Very Active, Athlete)[cite: 2].
* **Interactive Filters:** Global filters for **Age** and **Gender** to slice and dice analytics dynamically[cite: 2].
